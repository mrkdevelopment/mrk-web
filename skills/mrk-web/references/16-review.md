# Step 16: Review ✋

**Purpose.** The owner knows things about their business that no crawl or scorecard can. This is where they
catch the wrong phone number, the service they stopped offering, and the photo of someone who left.

## Hand over

Open the preview for them, inside the app if you can, and give them the address so they can open it on
their own phone too. Then a short checklist to work through:

1. **Facts.** Phone, email, address, hours, prices, names. Is every one right?
2. **Every page.** Open each from the menu. Anything missing, or that should not be there?
3. **Forms.** Send a test from each. Did it arrive where you expected?
4. **Your phone.** Scroll the home page top to bottom. Anything awkward?
5. **Does it sound like you?** Read the first screen of the home page and one service page aloud.
6. **Would you be proud to send a customer here?** If not, what is in the way?

Add `_mrk/owner-todo.md` so they can supply what only they have, and `_mrk/scores/compare.md` so they see what
was gained.

For an agency: the agency reviews first, then the client. Record both.

## Take the feedback

Write every comment into `_mrk/review.md` as a list, each with a status: fixed, will not change (with the
reason, agreed with them), or needs a decision.

- Fix in one batch, redeploy the preview, and send back the list with what changed.
- If a request breaks a rule (inventing a testimonial, a claim they cannot support), explain why and offer the
  honest alternative.
- If a request would drop the site below 98 or fail a scorecard check, say what it costs and let them choose.
- Add each change under `## Unreleased` in the changelog.

Repeat until they approve. There is no limit on rounds, but batch comments rather than fixing one at a time.

## Approval

Approval is the owner saying so in their own words. Silence, or "looks good so far", is not approval.
Record the words and the date in `_mrk/review.md`.

**Done when:** approval is recorded and every comment has a status.
