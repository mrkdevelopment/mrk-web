# House style

MRK's defaults for sites built to win enquiries from business owners. They come from reviewing real service
pages and seeing what leaked visitors or lost trust.

**These are defaults, not rules.** Edit this file to suit your own studio. On any one build, the brief and
the owner override it; note the departure under `decisions` in `_mrk/state.json`.

## Page order

- **The enquiry form comes before anything that sends people away.** Tools, articles, videos, case studies
  and links to them all sit below the form. Links out before the form leak the visitors the page exists for.
- **No button that points to the page you are on.** On the contact page, the header, footer and hero do not
  link to "Contact". The form is the action.
- **One main action, one visibly secondary one.** If "send an enquiry" is the main action, "book a call" is
  a quieter card beside it, not a rival button.

## Sections

- **FAQs in two columns on desktop**, one on phones. Real questions only.
- **No breadcrumb trail in a layered page header.** It clutters. Keep the breadcrumb in the page's structured
  data instead.

## Colour

- **Brand colours only.** No red, amber or green to mark "bad" and "good" figures unless the brand has them.
- Light text on a bright brand-colour panel is often hard to read. Test it; prefer white or the darkest brand
  colour as the panel.
- The one exception: other companies' marks appear in their own colours.

## Logos and names

- **Real logos for real services.** When a page names a tool, partner or platform, show its actual logo. With
  no real logo to hand, show no icon at all rather than a generic one.
- Name things at the level a customer knows: the company, not its product line.
- Social networks are "platforms", not "tools".

## Words

- **Business language, not technical language.** Owners do not want to copy and paste code. Say "the technical
  side, done for you" rather than naming the technology.
- **Figures must make sense to a stranger.** A client name nobody knows means nothing in a headline. Lead with
  the outcome in plain words: "One client's home page went from 2.1 seconds to under a third of a second."
- Say what happens after the form is sent, and how soon.

## Before calling a page done

Scan it top to bottom: is there any link away from the page above the form? Any button pointing at the page
itself? Any colour that is not in the design system? Any generic icon standing in for a real logo?
