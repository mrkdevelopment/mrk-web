# Step 20: What next

**Purpose.** A launch is the start of knowing what works. This step leaves the owner with a short, dated
plan instead of a finished-looking site that slowly goes stale.

## Write `_mrk/roadmap.md`

Keep it to one page. Every item has an owner (them, or "ask MRK-Web") and a date.

### First 30 days: protect what was moved

- **Weekly**: Search Console's Pages report for "not found" errors and pages that dropped out. Each one is a
  missing redirect or a lost page: fix and re-release.
- Compare clicks and impressions by page with the baseline from step 2. A page that fell needs a look.
- Confirm enquiries are arriving, and ask whether their quality changed.
- Close anything still open in `_mrk/redirects.csv` and `_mrk/owner-todo.md`.
- Cancel the old hosting only after two clean weeks.

### By 60 days: fill the gaps

- **Proof to collect**: the reviews, results and photos the scorecard wanted but the business did not have.
  Give the owner wording for asking customers.
- **Pages competitors have and this site lacks**, from the competitor notes in the facts sheet.
- **Off-site listings** from step 10: Google Business Profile, directories, review sites.
- Real photos to replace anything generated in step 8.

### By 90 days: improve

- Re-run the scorecard and the speed test. Record the scores beside the launch ones.
- The two or three changes most likely to bring more enquiries, each as a small versioned release.
- Features worth considering for this business: online booking, a quote calculator, a reviews feed, more
  location or service pages, a newsletter.
- **Editing without code**: if the owner wants to change content themselves, a content system can sit behind
  this same site. MRK-Web 2.0 will add headless WordPress and EmDash CMS as options.

## Also record

- Shortcuts taken during the build and what would remove them (`ponytail-debt` lists them if installed).
- How to make a change: say `continue` or describe it, work on a new version branch, review on the preview,
  release.
- When the next check-in is. Suggest a quarterly re-score.

## Skills

`seo-plan` for a fuller search strategy, `seo-drift` to catch regressions, `improve-animations` and
`ponytail-debt` for upkeep.

## Hand back

Finish with the hand-back in `SKILL.md`: scores old and new, the live address and repo, the owner's to-do
list, and this roadmap.

**Done when:** `_mrk/roadmap.md` exists and the owner knows what happens next and when.
