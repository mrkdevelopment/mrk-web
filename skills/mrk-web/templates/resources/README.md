# Your materials

Put anything here that you want the new website to use or be guided by. The more that is real and yours, the
closer the first design will be to what you want.

You can add files at any time. Nothing here is changed or deleted by the build, and nothing is published
unless you say it should be.

| Folder | Put here | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `brand/` | Your brand kit. **What is here is used as given.** | Logo files, fonts, colours, brand guidelines |
| `photos/` | Real photos and video files | Your team, your work, your premises, your products |
| `documents/` | Anything written | Brochures, product PDFs, price lists, a brief, notes, text you want on the site |
| `links.md` | Links | YouTube videos, social profiles, review pages, booking links |
| `inspiration/` | How you want it to look and work | Sites you like, screenshots, things to avoid |
| `search-console/` | Your Google Search Console pages report | The export that shows which pages Google sends people to |

## Brand kit: `brand/`

- `brand.md`: fill in what you know (colours, fonts, how you sound). Leave the rest blank.
- `logos/`: every version you have. A vector file (SVG, AI, EPS or PDF) is best; otherwise the largest PNG.
- `fonts/`: font files you have a licence to use on a website. Or just name the fonts in `brand.md`.
- `guidelines/`: your brand guidelines document, if you have one.

Your brand choices win. If your brand uses a particular font or colour, the site uses it, even where our
usual advice would pick differently.

## Photos: `photos/`

Real photos beat perfect ones. Phone photos are fine. Subfolders help but are not required
(`team`, `work`, `premises`, `products`). Name people in the file name if they should be named on the site.

Long videos are better uploaded to YouTube, with the link added to `links.md`.

## Documents: `documents/`

Anything that says what you do, for whom, and what it costs. PDF, Word or plain text.

If a document should be **downloadable from the site** (a product sheet, a brochure), say so. Everything else
is read for facts only and stays private.

## Search Console: `search-console/`

If the current site has Google Search Console, its Pages report tells us which pages matter most, so none of
them is lost. The steps to export it are in the README inside that folder. It takes about two minutes.

## Inspiration: `inspiration/`

- `sites.md`: sites you like, and a line on what you like about each. Sites you dislike are just as useful.
- `screenshots/`: screenshots of pages or sections you like, from any website.
- `patterns/`: specific pieces you want on your site: a pricing table, a booking form, a gallery, a menu.
- `avoid/`: examples of what you do not want.

We borrow structure and ideas from inspiration, never someone else's words, pictures or branding.
