### Analytics (only if you agree)

If you press **Accept** on our cookie banner, we use **Google Analytics** to understand how many people visit, which pages they read and how they found us. Google Analytics sets cookies (named `_ga` and `_ga_…`) to do this. We have turned off Google's advertising features and Google signals.

If you press **Decline**, or ignore the banner, Google Analytics does not load and sets no cookies. You can change your mind at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page; declining removes the analytics cookies. Your choice is stored in your browser for 12 months.

Google's privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Hosting

Our website is hosted by **Cloudflare** (Cloudflare Pages). Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks. We do not use this information to identify you.

- **Australia:** check whether the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles apply (many small businesses under the turnover threshold are exempt, but some types of business are not). The regulator is the OAIC: https://www.oaic.gov.au
- **United Kingdom / European Union:** UK GDPR or the EU GDPR usually applies to visitors there. The policy will need the lawful basis for each use (for enquiries, usually legitimate interests or steps before a contract; for analytics, consent), the right to complain to the regulator (ICO in the UK: https://ico.org.uk), and details of transfers outside the UK/EU. Cookie consent rules (PECR in the UK, the ePrivacy rules in the EU) are why analytics waits for Accept.
- **United States:** there is no single federal privacy law. Check state laws such as California's (CCPA/CPRA) if the business meets their thresholds, and the FTC's guidance on telling people the truth about data use: https://www.ftc.gov
